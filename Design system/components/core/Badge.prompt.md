**Badge** — a compact status or category pill; soft washed tint by default, solid fill for high-emphasis live states.

```jsx
<Badge tone="solar" icon={<i data-lucide="sun" />}>Producing</Badge>
<Badge tone="battery" variant="solid">Charging</Badge>
<Badge tone="danger">Fault</Badge>
<Badge tone="neutral">Idle</Badge>
```

Tones: `solar | battery | grid | home | danger | neutral`. Variants: `soft | solid`.
